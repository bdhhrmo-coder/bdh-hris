Exchange of Duty Rules

Checked for each employee on the duty they take over and their nearest duties before and after it. An exchange is refused for: straight duty (no gap or an overlap); a PM duty followed by an AM duty the next day; or less rest than set below after the duty just finished (for any other duty length, rest equal to its length). More duty days in a row than the employee's pattern is a warning only. Both duties must be the same length. Changes apply to requests filed from then on.
