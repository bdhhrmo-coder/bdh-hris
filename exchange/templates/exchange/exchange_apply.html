Request duty exchange

One-for-one only: you take the other employee's date, they take yours. The other employee must consent before this is submitted for endorsement.

Which kind of exchange is this? The system decides from that month's duty schedule:
  • Regular — the schedule is not submitted yet. File at least 7 days before the schedule cut-off and pick both shifts. Once approved, it is filled into the supervisor's draft.
  • Change to an approved schedule — at least 24 hours' notice. The shifts are read from the schedule.
  • Emergency — tick "Documented emergency"; same day allowed, written follow-up within 24 hours.
While the schedule is under review (submitted, not yet approved), only an emergency exchange can be filed.
