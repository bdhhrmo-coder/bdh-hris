Positions and Exchange Groups

Exchange of Duty partners must be in the same section and hold positions in the same Exchange group. Both positions in the same group: allowed. Different groups, or one in a group and the other in none: not allowed. Neither in a group: allowed (same section only). A change applies to requests filed from then on.

Positions are listed from the titles typed on employee records. Titles that differ only in capitals or spacing are one position; any other difference is a separate position, so give each variant its group.
