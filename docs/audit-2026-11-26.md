- OPTION B: keep the automatic remark (typed remark optional).
- RECOMMENDED: A. It is the later instruction, and it makes the OIC think about the second step.

- OPTION A: keep equal length only, as recorded in CLAUDE.md on Nov 26; longer coverage is filed as OT.

- RECOMMENDED: A for the pilot. It matches §8 "never generates extra OT, CTO or pay". Revisit B after the pilot if units need it.

- OPTION B: only Nurse, Nursing Attendant and Midwife, as the checklist says.
- RECOMMENDED: A. HR can switch any group off, and the extra three stop cross-profession swaps in the Laboratory, Radiology and Pharmacy.

**DECISION NEEDED 4 — What the TPE weights apply to**
- OPTION A: the Design Package, as built. Rank-and-file Performance 50 / Supervisor 30 / Peer 10 / Attendance 10; supervisors Objective 40 / Top Management 20 / Subordinates 30 / Peers 10.

- RECOMMENDED: B.

**DECISION NEEDED 6 — Who answers Q1–Q3**

- RECOMMENDED: A.
